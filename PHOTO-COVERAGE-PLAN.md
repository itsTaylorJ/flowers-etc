## Simple shop photo recipe

For each priority product, take four photographs before delivery:

1. Straight-on full arrangement
2. Three-quarter view showing depth
3. Close-up of the best flowers and finishing details
4. Scale view on a clean table or held naturally

Use the phone’s original camera files rather than Facebook-downloaded copies. Photograph near soft window light, avoid filters and portrait-mode blur, remove workroom clutter, and leave breathing room around the arrangement. A vertical 4:5 photograph at least 1600 pixels wide is the best reusable master for the website and social media.
