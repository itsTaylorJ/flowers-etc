Our Story

Lisa Thompson has had flowers in her hands for most of her life. She got her start in a flower shop back in high school — and somewhere between the buckets of fresh roses and the spools of ribbon, arranging flowers stopped being a job and became the thing she loved.

Life bloomed in other ways too. Lisa stepped away from the shop to raise her two children as a stay-at-home mom — years she'll tell you were her most important work. When she came back to flowers, it was to the shop she knew best, working side by side for many years with its longtime owner. So when the day came to pass the shop along, there was never any question whose hands it belonged in. Lisa bought Flowers Etc., and she's never looked back.

Today, that lifetime of care goes into every arrangement that leaves the shop — the birthdays and the weddings, and the hard goodbyes too — each one made the way she'd make it for her own family. Because around here, that's usually exactly who it's for: neighbors, friends, and family.

Why Shop Local?

When you order through a big national flower website, your order gets sold to the nearest shop anyway — minus a big cut for the middleman. When you order from us directly, every dollar goes into fresher flowers and fuller arrangements.

Plus, we answer our own phone. Try getting that from an 800 number.
