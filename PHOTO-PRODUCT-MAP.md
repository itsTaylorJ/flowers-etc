## Photo standards going forward

1. Prefer work created by Lisa/current staff, or clearly label older images as Flowers Etc. archive work.
2. Use recent work first. A practical default is the last 24–36 months, while older exceptional images may remain if they still match current quality and style.
3. Avoid screenshots, heavy Facebook compression, cluttered worktables, people in the background, watermarks, and duplicate angles.
4. Capture a clean front view, a close detail, and one scale/context view.
5. Keep original files in the source archive; make separate web-sized copies.
6. Do not name or price a one-off arrangement as a repeatable product until Lisa confirms sourcing, labor, and minimum selling price.
