## Delivery

- Service area: Canton and addresses within 35 miles.
- Regular nearby towns: Mabank, Wills Point, Van, Grand Saline, Martins Mill, and Ben Wheeler.
- Fees: $5 inside Canton city limits; $10 just outside Canton city limits; $15 farther away within the 35-mile service area.
- No minimum flower purchase for delivery.
- Same-day cutoff: 2:30 PM Monday–Friday and 10:00 AM Saturday. Same-day service may be advertised with the cutoff and availability qualification.
- Saturday deliveries run during the shop's 8:00 AM–12:00 PM hours.
- Customers may request a specific delivery time. The shop confirms the request.
- Homes, businesses, schools, hospitals, funeral homes, churches, nursing homes, and assisted-living facilities may receive deliveries, subject to any facility rules.
- Funeral-home timing is coordinated directly with the funeral home. Normal delivery cutoff applies unless the shop agrees to a special arrangement.
- Cemetery orders require cemetery and grave-location information.

## Missed recipient and weather

- Try to contact the customer or recipient first.
- Leave the order safely when conditions allow.
- If the order cannot be left safely, contact the customer and arrange the next step.
- Porch delivery is allowed, but judgment should be used during heat, cold, rain, or wind.
