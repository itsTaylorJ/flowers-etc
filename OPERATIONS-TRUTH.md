## Delivery

- Service area: Canton and addresses within 35 miles.
- Regular nearby towns: Mabank, Wills Point, Van, Grand Saline, Martins Mill, and Ben Wheeler.
- Fees: $5 inside Canton city limits; $10 just outside Canton city limits; $15 farther away within the 35-mile service area.
- No minimum flower purchase for delivery.
- Same-day cutoff: 2:30 PM Monday–Friday and 10:00 AM Saturday. Same-day service may be advertised with the cutoff and availability qualification.
- Saturday deliveries run during the shop's 8:00 AM–12:00 PM hours.
- Customers may request a specific delivery time. The shop confirms the request.
- Homes, businesses, schools, hospitals, funeral homes, churches, nursing homes, and assisted-living facilities may receive deliveries. Flowers Etc. has no additional shop-specific restrictions; the receiving facility's own rules still apply.
- Delivery or placement photos are available by request.
- Funeral orders should be placed at least 24 hours ahead and delivered at least one hour before the funeral. Shorter-notice exceptions may be possible depending on the order, size, and availability; customers should call to confirm.
- Cemetery orders require cemetery and grave-location information.

## Weddings

- Wedding orders are preferred at least one month ahead.
- A 50% deposit is required up front.
- The remaining balance is due two weeks before the wedding date.
- Consultation cost/process, delivery/setup scope, and cancellation/refund terms remain unresolved.

## Cemetery Replacement

- Cemetery arrangements are $50 each.
- Scheduled placement is available at cemeteries within the shop's regular 35-mile delivery area.
- Customers choose the requested placement frequency and schedule.
- No additional delivery or placement fee is added to the $50 arrangement price.
- Photo confirmation is available by request.
- Flowers are sprayed with a weather protectant.
- Automatic replacement for arrangements that are removed, stolen, vandalized, weather-damaged, or otherwise damaged after placement is not included.
- If a customer or family member notices a problem after placement, they should call the shop. The team will review what happened and discuss the best available next step case by case.
- During a scheduled placement, the shop may remove or dispose of the previous arrangement when necessary.

## Additional event services

- Subject to date and availability: birthdays, quinceañeras, corporate events, private parties, baby showers, bridal showers, and holiday or milestone celebrations.

## Confirmed catalog pricing

- Classic Rose Bouquet: Half Dozen $55, Dozen $85, Two Dozen $165. Red, pink, yellow, and white roses are normally stocked; other colors require at least 24 hours notice for special ordering.

## Missed recipient and weather

- Try to contact the customer or recipient first.
- Leave the order safely when conditions allow.
- If the order cannot be left safely, contact the customer and arrange the next step.
- Porch delivery is allowed, but judgment should be used during heat, cold, rain, or wind.
